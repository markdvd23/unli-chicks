# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Filipino families, friend groups (barkadas), and value-conscious diners looking for a filling unlimited meal at a reasonable price. Context: they're deciding where to eat for a group outing — checking the site on mobile, comparing options, wanting to see what they get and where the nearest branch is before committing.

## Product Purpose

Unli Chix is a Filipino unlimited dining restaurant. One fixed price per person buys unlimited wings, fried chicken, fries, milktea, chaofan, ramen, and silog. The site exists to convert someone who's never been into a first visit — communicating abundance, value, and Filipino-casual warmth.

## Brand Personality

Generous, vibrant, Filipino-proud. Voice: the kind of place that says "kumain ka na?" the moment you walk in. Not fine-dining restraint, not fast-food cheerfulness — festive communal eating energy.

## Anti-references

- Upscale/minimalist Filipino restaurant branding (Toyo Eatery aesthetic — wrong audience register)
- Generic "modern fast food" red-and-yellow chains
- SaaS-cream landing pages transposed onto food

## Design Principles

1. Show the food. The proposition is abundance; images carry more weight than any copy line.
2. Filipino first. Language, rhythm, and cultural references come before generic marketing English.
3. Generosity over restraint. Big portions, big type, big photography. This is not a subtle brand.
4. Mobile is the primary device. Group dinner decisions are made on phones, often in the group chat.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion support required (GSAP matchMedia already implemented). All food images need descriptive alt text. Phone numbers must be tap-to-call on mobile.
